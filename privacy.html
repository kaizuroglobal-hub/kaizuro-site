Privacy

Personal information, handled with purpose.

This policy explains what information KAIZURO collects, how it is used, who may receive it and how you can request access, correction or deletion.

1. Information collected

KAIZURO may collect your name, email address, telephone number, delivery address, country, product preferences, order and payment references, communications, warranty information and feedback you choose to provide.

We may also collect limited technical information when you visit the website, such as browser type, device information, referring pages and general usage data, where enabled by the website or hosting provider.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • manage Founder allocations, orders, payments and delivery;
  • respond to enquiries and provide customer or warranty support;
  • send production, product and delivery updates;
  • improve KAIZURO products, services and website content;
  • prevent fraud, protect legal rights and comply with applicable law.

3. Service providers and disclosure

KAIZURO may share information with service providers that perform functions such as payment processing, email delivery, website hosting, manufacturing coordination, freight, accounting, customer support or professional advice.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect safety or legal rights, or in connection with a business restructuring or transfer.

4. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party providers such as Square. KAIZURO does not intend to store complete payment-card numbers. Payment providers handle payment information under their own privacy and security terms.

5. Marketing communications

KAIZURO may send product, Founder 100 or development updates where you have requested them or where otherwise permitted by law. You may unsubscribe using the instructions in the message or by contacting KAIZURO.

Operational messages relating to an order, payment, production milestone, warranty claim or delivery may still be sent where required to provide the service.

6. Storage and security

KAIZURO takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Information may be stored or processed in Australia or other countries used by KAIZURO's service providers. Where information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted or de-identified, subject to legal and record-keeping obligations.

7. Access, correction and deletion

You may request access to personal information KAIZURO holds about you, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, or request deletion where legally and operationally appropriate.

KAIZURO may need to verify your identity before processing a request and may retain information required for orders, tax, fraud prevention, warranties or legal obligations.

8. Cookies, links and website services

The KAIZURO website may use essential browser storage, cookies or third-party services needed for site operation, analytics, forms, payments or embedded content. External sites are governed by their own privacy policies.

9. Questions and complaints

Privacy enquiries, access requests or complaints should be sent to info@kaizuro.com. Please explain the request and provide enough information for KAIZURO to identify the relevant records.
