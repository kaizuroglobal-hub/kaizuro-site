Engineering Intent

Designed to cast.Built to load.Engineered torecover.

ASSAULT is the first physical expression of the KAIZURO philosophy: remove unnecessary compromise, prove the structure and refine the complete rod as one mechanical system.

Blank architecture, guide progression, grip geometry and reel retention are developed together around high-output topwater casting and the violent opening phase of a serious offshore fight.

Performance is judged by how the complete system behaves — not by one material name or one headline number.
01

Cast efficiently

Guide progression and recovery are developed to control braid cleanly through repeated high-output casting.

02

Carry load progressively

The working section is designed to build authority without turning the rod into a blunt, unforgiving lever.

03

Control under pressure

Grip geometry, torsional stability and reel retention work together when the fight becomes physical.
