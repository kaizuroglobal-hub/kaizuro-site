Engineering Intent

More pressure.More stability.Same control.

HALO takes the KAIZURO engineering philosophy into a heavier offshore platform. The objective is not simply more stiffness or more headline power, but a complete system that remains usable when the load environment becomes extreme.

Blank architecture, guide progression, grip geometry and reel retention are being developed around giant GT, very large tuna and the prolonged pressure of expedition fishing.

HALO will move forward only when recovery, torsional stability and load control work together as one mechanical system.
01

Recover under load

Recovery speed matters when repeated high-output casting and violent direction changes place the working section under pressure.

02

Stay torsionally stable

Guide train, blank structure and reel interface must resist unwanted twist as line angle and fish direction change.

03

Carry extreme pressure

The objective is usable authority against giant pelagics without turning the rod into an unforgiving lever.
