Grip Geometry

World-first grip geometry.

The elongated upper grip combines a 90% rounded profile with a deliberate 10% pentagonal index. It preserves offshore comfort while providing tactile orientation, leverage and resistance to unwanted rotation under heavy load.

Longer usable leverage Supports changing hand positions through the fight. Indexed orientation Provides a tactile reference without looking at the grip. Controlled transitions Rounded corners retain comfort while preserving the faceted form.

Reel Interface

The mechanical connection point.

The reel seat is where rod, reel and angler become one load-bearing system. ASSAULT uses a heavy-duty Fuji DPS configuration selected for secure reel retention, offshore reliability and compatibility with large PE6-8 spinning reels.

Once installed, the reel foot occupies the seat opening and the principal Fuji branding is positioned on the underside.
