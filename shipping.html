Shipping and Delivery

Prepared carefully. Delivered clearly.

This policy explains how KAIZURO handles production orders, delivery estimates, freight charges, international shipments and transport issues.

Australia

Australian delivery inclusions or charges will be displayed on the product page, at checkout or in the written order confirmation before the customer is asked to complete payment.

Remote-area, oversized-item or special-handling surcharges may apply where disclosed before dispatch.

International delivery

International freight will be included only where expressly stated. Otherwise, KAIZURO will provide a freight quote or display the applicable amount before final payment or dispatch.

The recipient is responsible for import duties, taxes, customs charges, brokerage fees and local compliance costs unless KAIZURO expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

Production and dispatch timing

Founder rods and other pre-production products are not held as finished stock. Any delivery estimate begins after production, quality assurance and final payment milestones are completed.

Estimated dates are not guaranteed unless KAIZURO expressly confirms a guaranteed delivery date in writing. Customers will receive meaningful updates if timing changes.

Address accuracy

Customers must provide a complete and accurate delivery address, telephone number and any information reasonably required by the carrier.

Additional costs caused by an incorrect address, failed delivery, refused shipment or uncollected parcel may be charged to the customer where permitted by law.

Tracking and receipt

Where tracking is available, KAIZURO will provide the tracking reference after dispatch. Customers should inspect packaging and the rod promptly after delivery.

Visible transport damage should be photographed before the packaging is discarded and reported to KAIZURO as soon as reasonably possible.

Loss, delay or damage

KAIZURO will assist with carrier enquiries and valid transport claims. A carrier delay does not remove any rights a customer may have under Australian Consumer Law.

Where a product arrives damaged, is lost in transit or is not supplied as agreed, KAIZURO will assess the circumstances and provide the remedy required by law.

Split shipments and accessories

KAIZURO may ship a rod and its Founder Pack together or separately where packaging, production timing or freight protection makes this appropriate. Customers will be told if an order is divided into multiple shipments.

Change of delivery details

Delivery details may be changed before dispatch, subject to identity verification. Once a shipment has entered the carrier network, changes may not be possible and may attract additional carrier charges.

Need a freight estimate?

Send your country, postcode and preferred rod model. KAIZURO will confirm the current delivery approach before you complete the applicable final payment.
