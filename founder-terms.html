These terms explain how a KAIZURO Founder allocation is reserved, when production is activated, how payments are applied and what happens if production does not proceed.

1. Founder allocation

The KAIZURO Founder 100 is limited to 100 individually numbered rods across the ASSAULT PE6-8 and HALO PE10-12 models combined. A Founder position is not secured until the applicable deposit has been received and KAIZURO has confirmed the allocation.

Model quantities, colourways, accessories and Founder numbers are subject to availability. KAIZURO may close a model or option before all 100 total allocations are filled.

2. Deposit and payment

The deposit amount displayed on the Founder page and at checkout is credited in full against the final purchase price. The remaining balance is payable at the production milestone stated in KAIZURO's payment notice.

Payments are processed by the payment provider identified at checkout. A payment receipt does not override these terms or create an allocation where the programme or selected model has already closed.

3. Production activation

Founder production will be activated only after KAIZURO has completed the required product validation, confirmed the first-run specification and achieved the manufacturing and funding threshold required to place the production order.

KAIZURO will notify Founder customers when production has been formally activated. Production timing may be affected by component availability, testing, supplier scheduling, freight or events outside KAIZURO's reasonable control.

4. Pre-production specifications

Founder rods are pre-production products. Published measurements, components, finishes, weights and performance guidance may be refined before the production specification is locked.

KAIZURO may make reasonable changes that improve performance, safety, durability, availability or manufacturability. If a material change prevents KAIZURO from supplying the selected model or substantially changes the agreed product, the customer will be offered an alternative or a refund.

5. Refunds and cancellation

If KAIZURO does not proceed to Founder production, the Founder deposit will be refunded in full.

Before production activation, a customer may request cancellation and a refund unless the checkout or written allocation confirmation states otherwise. After production activation, deposits may be committed to manufacturing and are not refundable for change of mind, except where required by law or where KAIZURO agrees otherwise in writing.

Nothing in these terms limits rights relating to products that are faulty, not as described, unsafe or otherwise fail to meet applicable consumer guarantees.

6. Balance, delivery and ownership

The remaining balance must be paid by the due date stated in the payment notice. KAIZURO may cancel an allocation after reasonable notice if the balance remains unpaid.

Delivery charges, import duties, taxes and remote-area costs are governed by the KAIZURO Shipping Policy. Ownership transfers after full payment has cleared. Risk transfers in accordance with applicable law and the selected delivery service.

7. Founder identification and Founder Pack

Each confirmed Founder rod includes Founder identification, an allocated number, a Founder certificate and one original KAIZURO Founder Pack, subject to final production availability.

Only 100 original Founder Packs are intended to be produced, one for each Founder rod. KAIZURO may later sell standard versions of individual accessories, but they will not be represented as the original Founder Pack.

8. Questions and notices

Questions, cancellation requests and formal notices should be sent to info@kaizuro.com. Include your name, order reference and selected rod model.
