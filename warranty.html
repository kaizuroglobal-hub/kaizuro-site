Every KAIZURO rod is backed by a five-year limited manufacturing warranty for the original registered owner, together with dedicated Founder Priority Support for the first 100 owners.

What is covered

For five years from the original purchase date, KAIZURO covers confirmed defects in materials and workmanship affecting the rod blank, assembly or supplied components under normal intended use.

Where an eligible manufacturing defect is confirmed, KAIZURO will repair or replace the affected rod or component, or provide another appropriate remedy.

What is not covered

  • Accidental damage, impact, crushing or transport damage
  • High-sticking, misuse or use outside the rod's intended specifications
  • Unauthorised modification or repair
  • Normal wear, cosmetic marks or finish deterioration
  • Corrosion or damage caused by inadequate cleaning, storage or maintenance
  • Guide, tip or component damage caused by impact, line entanglement or external force

Founder Priority Support

Founder 100 owners receive priority warranty assessment and access to replacement-section or accidental-damage assistance where available.

This support does not create an unlimited free-replacement entitlement. Availability, assessment outcomes, shipping and any applicable replacement charge will be confirmed before work proceeds.

Your consumer rights

This written warranty is provided in addition to rights and remedies available under Australian Consumer Law. Those statutory rights cannot be excluded.

International customers may also have mandatory consumer rights under the laws applying in their location.

Make a claim

Start with the rod, the issue and the evidence.

Email KAIZURO with your name, proof of purchase, rod model and serial or Founder number, a clear description of the issue, and photographs showing the full rod and affected area. Do not ship a rod until KAIZURO provides written instructions.
